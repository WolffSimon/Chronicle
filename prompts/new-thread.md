# Adding a thread

Paste this in the master chat when a new area of work or life needs its own thread.

```text
I want a new thread for [area]. Its charter: [one or two sentences]. It must
never touch: [other threads' business].

1. Search the project for everything already held about [area]. Write
   [folder]/Record_Extract_[today].md from it, with a source for every item,
   and a first section listing where older documents are out of date.
2. Write [folder]/Thread_Context.md from templates/thread-context.md. Put
   anything you could not fill as a numbered question in section 11.
3. Start [folder]/Thread_Log.md with one entry recording the thread's
   creation, in my words.
4. If items for this area sit on the master list, list them for me. Ask me
   which should move to the new thread's list before you move any.
5. If you can, publish the thread's running list from running-list/index.html
   with its own CONFIG, and record the link in section 6 of the context.
6. Add the thread to the table in the project instructions and show me the
   new version. I will paste it in.
7. Give me the opening message for the thread's first chat.
```
