# A worked example

Alex is fictional. Alex is a deputy head at a secondary school and the secretary of a local allotment society. Alex's house also needs work. This shows the pattern at small scale, three weeks in.

## The thread table, from Alex's project instructions

| Thread | Charter, in one sentence | Folder |
|---|---|---|
| Master | Keeps the record, the people list and the master list, and folds the logs into them. | `master/` |
| School | Alex's work as deputy head: timetabling, the inspection, staff matters that are Alex's to handle. | `school/` |
| Allotments | Alex's work as secretary of the society: minutes, the waiting list, the council lease. | `allotments/` |
| Home | The house, the garden, the car and the family calendar. | `home/` |
