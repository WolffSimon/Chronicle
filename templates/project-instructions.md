## Canonical documents

These live in `master/`. The master chat keeps them. Other threads read them and propose changes through their logs.

- `House_Style.md`: the rules for any prose drafted for me. One copy only. Folders point to it.
- `Record.md`: the facts about me that documents may use, with consistency rules. Never invent a fact. Ask.
- `People.md`: the people in my life and work, with handling notes for each.

## Rules for every thread

- Read your thread's context first, then the house style, then the log, then the record extract.
- If your conversation begins with a summary of an earlier conversation, it has been compacted. Before your next reply, re-read your thread's context, the house style and your log, in that order.
- The log outranks older documents, and I outrank the folder.
- Record each new fact I give you the same day, in your log, dated, in my words.
- Never invent a fact, a number, a date or a name. Ask.
- Put open questions to me numbered, in one batch, each answerable in a word or a line.
- Ask before adding anything to a list that I have not asked for.
- When a thread retires or hands over, it writes an operating guide for its successor.
